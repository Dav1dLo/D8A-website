Cookie Policy

Last updated: April 7, 2026

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently and provide information to site owners.

2. Cookies We Use

D8A.pro is designed to be privacy-friendly. We currently minimize cookie usage:

CategoryPurposeRequired
EssentialBasic website functionality (no cookies currently required)N/A
AnalyticsPrivacy-friendly website analytics (cookieless when possible)Optional

We do not use advertising cookies, tracking cookies, or third-party cookies for behavioral profiling.

3. Third-Party Cookies

If we use a privacy-friendly analytics tool such as Plausible Analytics, it operates without cookies entirely. In this case, no third-party cookies are set by our Website.

4. Managing Cookies

You can control and manage cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to:

  • View what cookies are stored on your device
  • Delete individual or all cookies
  • Block cookies from specific or all websites
  • Block third-party cookies

Please note that blocking essential cookies may affect website functionality.

5. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by the “Last updated” date at the top of this page.
