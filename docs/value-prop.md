# D8A: value proposition (website V2)

**Reader:** Head of Data / data platform lead at a mid-to-large enterprise.
**Action:** join the waitlist.

## One line

**The autonomous enterprise: from business request to governed action, with your team signing off at each step.**

## The problem (in their words)

Every data request takes weeks. It passes from the business to an analyst, an architect, an engineer, a reviewer and a release manager. Context gets lost between hand-offs. The patterns that keep the platform consistent live in a few senior heads. The backlog grows faster than the team, and generic AI coding tools don't know your architecture.

## The promise

D8A turns what your business asks for into specs, specs into code on your stack, and data into decisions and actions. You approve each step before anything changes.

## How: four building blocks

| # | Block | What it does | Where the human decides |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | **Data**: specs are the new code | A business user describes what they need in plain language. D8A writes a reviewable spec grounded in your architecture. AI agents generate code that follows your patterns, from bronze to gold, on the platform you already run. | Business signs off the spec, an engineer reviews the pull request, and your own CI/CD releases it. |
| 02 | **Ontology**: your pointer for AI | Domains, entities, owners and lineage as one queryable model of your business. AI answers with your definitions of customer, contract or asset. | Each domain has one named owner. |
| 03 | **Intelligence**: what to do when | Decision logic tied to your data and processes: rules, ML models, code or agents. | You define the logic and the thresholds. |
| 04 | **Execution**: governed actions | Actions defined on the ontology (block a customer, update a record, call an API) run only through approved, logged paths. | Actions run with permissions and leave an audit record. |

**Governance** runs across all four: each step leaves a record of who approved what, and when.

## Why D8A

- **Built on your architecture.** Patterns, naming and layers live in a Blueprint, and generated code is checked against it.
- **Runs on your stack.** D8A has no engine of its own. Code lands in your Git and runs on your platform.
- **Your data stays home.** D8A works on metadata. Rows never leave your environment.
